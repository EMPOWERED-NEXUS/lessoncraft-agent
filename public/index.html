LessonCraft helps learners use trusted learning materials to check what they understand, see what remains unclear, and practice one useful next step. Pathways is the current learning experience.

  1. Choose a learning goal.
  2. Use approved evidence.
  3. See what is demonstrated or still unclear.
  4. Take one next practice step.

This prototype covers one workplace status-update skill. It does not cover every subject, a full curriculum, or a video course. No account is needed.
